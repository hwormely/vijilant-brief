The AI Difference

Not AI as a feature.
AI as the infrastructure.

RecoverPrepared does not integrate AI into its workflow. Vijilant's Auto-Triage Engine runs on every assessment save, every case note, every task completion — surfacing the right information to the right person at the right moment.

Triage that updates itself

Every time a case note is saved, a task is completed, or a referral outcome is logged, the Auto-Triage Engine re-evaluates the client's tier. Critical cases don't stay buried in a flat list — they surface automatically.

AI-written rationale

The system doesn't just assign a tier — it writes the rationale. Assessment Part B is generated automatically on save: official Triage Tier (T1–T4), Recovery Score (0–1000), 5 domain scores, and a plain-language explanation of why. CMs read it. They don't write it.

Recovery Plan generated on save

When a 12-section assessment is completed, the system computes a Recovery Score (0–1000) across 5 domains and generates a full, personalized Recovery Plan — Housing, Financial, Health, Legal, Emotional — without the CM drafting a single line. It's ready to review in seconds.

HIPAA-grade from the ground up

HIPAA-certified infrastructure with enterprise BAA. Every write to a client record is logged in a full audit trail before any protected health information enters the system. Compliance isn't a feature we added — it's a precondition for launching.

What does an auto-generated Recovery Plan look like?

After the CM completes the 12-section clinical assessment, the system computes five domain scores and writes a structured Recovery Plan. No drafting. No templates to fill. The CM reviews, annotates, and approves — the system wrote the first draft.

Domain outputs: Housing Stability · Financial Recovery · Physical Health · Legal & Benefits · Emotional & Social Wellbeing. Domain values display as readable labels (None / Disrupted / Partial / Stable / Strong). The 5 domain scores combine into the Recovery Score (0–1000), visualized throughout the app as a progress ring on every client record.

Honest Assessment

Where we're still building.

Vijilant is purpose-built and field-tested in concept — but v1 is the beginning. These are real gaps we're closing methodically, not afterthoughts.

Survivor-facing portal — planned post-v1

Client self-service access to their own case records is on the roadmap. v1 is optimized for CM and supervisor workflows first. Survivor portal comes in the business-enterprise phase with appropriate consent and security infrastructure.

No production deployment at scale yet

Vijilant is in active development and currently validated against LLP's real caseload context. First production deployment is the LLP pilot. RecoverPrepared has more field time — that matters and we respect it.

AI rationale is only as good as the assessment input

The Auto-Triage Engine relies on the quality and completeness of assessment data entered by CMs. Incomplete assessments produce lower-confidence triage outputs. This is disclosed in the system and surfaced in the UI.

Multi-organization licensing is phase 2

The architecture supports multi-tenancy from day one, but the licensing and onboarding infrastructure for external DCM organizations is a post-LLP-pilot initiative. We are not promising it as an immediate deliverable.

Auto-Assignment + Alerts

Matching resources appear on the client record. Admins receive urgency alerts when resource categories run low.

The same meta-tagging system that assigns resources to clients also works in reverse: when multiple clients are flagged as needing a resource category that has no active entries in the directory, the system notifies the relevant admin that a resource gap exists and needs to be filled. This closes the loop between frontline casework and organizational resource planning — something no current DCM platform automates.

What the resource directory looks like today

Even in v1, CMs and admins can browse and manage the resource library from within the app. Resources are organized by category — Housing, Financial, Health, Legal, Benefits — and are accessible from any client's case file.

In v2, this directory becomes the engine that drives auto-assignment. The v1 structure is already built to support that transition without a schema change.
