Survivor Portal — Design Note: This is the client-facing view of Vijilant. Survivors access their own case using a secure link sent by their CM. The portal is read-optimized — survivors can see their status, score, recovery plan, and aid applications. They can message their CM and upload documents. They can add their signature to aid applications requiring it. No PHI editing, no assessment input, no CM tools.
